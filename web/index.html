blep

Find your lost Bluetooth things — guided by feel. blep turns your phone into a warm/cold pointer that walks you right to it. The same scan runs in reverse to catch unwanted trackers following you — and points you to them too.

Android · now in testing

To join Android testing, become a tester via our Google Group, then opt in on Play. iPhone & iPad coming soon.

Free & open source · companion apps for Apple Watch & Wear OS

1

Calibrate

Hold the phone to your chest. Your body shields the signal from behind, making it directional.

2

Sweep & walk

Turn slowly until it’s warmest, then walk forward. The arrow and colour guide every step.

3

Pinpoint

Up close, kneel and search low. blep celebrates the moment you find it.

See it in action

More than a finder

The same scan that finds your things also protects you from someone else's. All of it on-device — no account, no cloud, no ads.

🛡

Anti-tracking scan

Catches an AirTag, Tile or SmartTag travelling with you — then walks you right to it so you can remove it.

 🔁

Sees through rotating IDs

Privacy trackers change their Bluetooth address constantly. blep recognises the same physical device anyway.

 🌙

Watches in the background

Off, a battery-friendly timer, or a continuous watch — your choice, with a notification when something's off.

 ⌚

On your wrist

Companion apps for Wear OS & Apple Watch. On Android, phone and watch stay in sync — names, favourites, alerts.

 🎒

Left-behind alerts

Tether your bag, keys or wallet and get a nudge the moment you walk away without them.

 🔒

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no internet. Scans, sensors and history never leave your device.

Help & donate

blep is free and open source. If it reunited you with your keys (or your dignity), a small thank-you keeps the lights on. Pick whatever’s easiest:

Card · Stripe Quick & secure card payment Open Collective PDF receipt · transparent ledger PayPal Record in your PayPal history GitHub Sponsors Best for recurring support
Need a receipt?

Contributions are voluntary support, not tax-deductible donations (blep isn't a registered non-profit), so a formal donation certificate can't be issued. For a downloadable PDF, use Open Collective — it puts a receipt on your contribution page. PayPal and GitHub Sponsors keep a payment record in your account history.
