## Description

```
blep turns your phone into a warm/cold pointer that walks you right to your lost
Bluetooth things — keys with a tag, earbuds, a speaker, a watch, your car. The
same scan runs in reverse to catch unwanted trackers travelling with you (AirTag,
Tile, SmartTag, Find My / Google Find My Device) — and, because blep is a finder,
it points you straight to them.

No map. No account. No ads. No tracking. Everything happens on your device.

▶ JOIN THE ANDROID TEST (closed testing)

iPhone & iPad — coming soon.

WHAT IT DOES
• Find by signal — a compass-and-signal pointer, not a fuzzy radar; a warm/cold
  trail and turn-by-turn hints ("straight ahead · ~8 m").
• "Is something tracking you?" — detects unwanted trackers following you, catches
  the ones that rotate their Bluetooth ID, and flags a tag that keeps reappearing
  across separate places/contexts (not just your daily commute).
• Find the tracker — it doesn't just warn; it walks you to it so you can remove it.
• Background watch, Relaxed/Balanced/Strict sensitivity, optional coarse
  on-device location — all opt-in.
• Companion apps for Apple Watch and Wear OS.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no analytics, no ads. Your Bluetooth scans and motion sensing never
leave the phone. Free and open source.
```
